Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

WishPlay collects basic information necessary to sync your game wishlist across devices:

  • Account Information: Email address and display name when signing in via Google Sign-In or email.
  • App Data: Games added to your personal wishlist and your notification preferences.
  • Device Information: App version and anonymous diagnostics to help troubleshoot technical issues.

2. Payment Information

We do not collect or store your payment card numbers, bank accounts, or billing security codes. All payments are processed directly by Paddle.com (our Merchant of Record) under PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

3. How We Use Information

We use your information exclusively to provide wishlist synchronization, alert you to game release dates, and verify your Pro account entitlement.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell your personal data. We only share information with service providers required to operate WishPlay (e.g., Google Firebase for database storage and Paddle for payment verification).

5. Contact Us

For any privacy inquiries or to request data deletion, contact us at mdliad.se@gmail.com.

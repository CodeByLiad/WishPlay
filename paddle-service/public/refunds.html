Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want you to be completely satisfied with WishPlay Pro. If you are not satisfied with your purchase for any reason, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your purchase date.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, simply send an email to mdliad.se@gmail.com with:

  • Your registered WishPlay email address
  • Your Paddle transaction ID (found in your receipt email)

3. Processing Time

Refunds are processed within 3 business days by Paddle. Once issued, the funds typically appear back on your original payment method (card or PayPal) within 5-10 business days depending on your bank.

4. Revocation of Pro Access

Upon a completed refund, your account will automatically revert from WishPlay Pro to the standard free tier without affecting your saved games or wishlists.

# Platform icons

Platform marks are adapted from the Simple Icons CC0 collection into Android vector drawables. The PC monitor uses Material Icons supplied by the existing Android Compose dependency. Platform names remain beside the decorative marks; PS4/PS5 and Switch/Switch 2 share their respective brand mark and retain distinct text labels.

- PlayStation, Android, Apple: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/14.15.0/icons
- Xbox, Nintendo Switch: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/10.0.0/icons
- Upstream license: [Simple Icons CC0](simple-icons.md)

Brand names and trademarks belong to their respective owners. These icons identify supported platforms and do not imply endorsement.
